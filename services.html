Lodge Accommodation & Surf Services

1. Full Board Surf Eco-Bungalows

Traditional sago-palm thatched bungalows featuring ocean views, mosquito netting, power charging stations, and three daily fresh seafood and farm meals.

2. Guided Reef Break & Boat Charters

Daily boat transfers and local surf guides to Lido Right, Lido Left, Sarp Point, Town Stop, and Narimo Island breaks tailored to your experience level.

3. Expat Weekend Getaway Special

Designed for PNG-based expatriates and business travelers seeking quick weekend surf trips via direct Air Niugini flights from Port Moresby.
