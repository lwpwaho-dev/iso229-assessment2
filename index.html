Welcome to Vanimo Surf Lodge

Situated directly beachfront at Lido Village in West Sepik (Sandaun) Province, Vanimo Surf Lodge offers uncrowded, premier tropical reef breaks. Whether you are an international surf tourist, an expat based in PNG, or a local wave rider, our lodge provides sustainable beachfront accommodation and direct access to legendary waves like Lido Right, Lido Left, and Sarp Point.

Home of the Annual Vanimo Surf Pro

Premier Surf Destination

During the northern swells from November through April, Vanimo becomes the epicenter of wave riding in Papua New Guinea. Consistent 3-to-10 foot waves roll right up to our doorstep, creating ideal conditions for both longboards and shortboards.

Community & Cultural Tourism

Every year, the lodge hosts the Annual Vanimo Surf Pro & Cultural Festival. The event brings together local village surfers, Port Moresby and Lae expats, and international surf adventurers for three days of competitive surf heats, traditional sing-sings, and beachfront feasts.
